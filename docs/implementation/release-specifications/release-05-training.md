# Release R5 - Training and adult development

## Objective

Provide approved adult training and development records.

## Scope

- Course and category catalog
- Training sessions and venues
- Applications and selection
- Attendance and evaluation
- Certificates and expiry
- Training history and development pathways

## Dependencies

- R1 member identity
- R2 mandate and role model
- Approved eligibility and completion rules
- Participant data and certificate policy

## Exit criteria

- Only authorized staff create or modify training records
- Selection and completion evidence is retained
- Certificates follow approved rules
- Participant and certificate exports are scoped
