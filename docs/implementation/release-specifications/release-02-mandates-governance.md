# Release R2 - Mandates and governance

## Objective

Represent offices, appointments, delegations and statutory governance
workflows with effective authorization and history.

## Scope

- Position catalog
- Appointment and mandate lifecycle
- Delegations with scope and expiry
- Combined-office and conflict rules if approved
- Statutory bodies, meetings, decisions and minutes
- Mandate reporting

## Dependencies

- R1 structure and member identity
- Approved nomination and term rules
- Role/scope matrix
- Competent-authority and meeting rules

## Exit criteria

- Every mandate retains complete effective history
- Expiry/revocation removes or reduces scope immediately
- Delegation cannot exceed source authority
- Governance actions pass approved authority checks
- Meeting and minutes workflows record authorization and evidence
