# Release R7 - Strategic plans and reporting

## Objective

Connect approved plans, projects and periodic reporting to source records.

## Scope

- Strategic priorities and annual plans
- Projects and conventions
- Partners and intervention areas
- Actions, deliverables and indicators
- Periodic report validation and revision
- Territorial and strategic dashboards

## Dependencies

- R1 membership/structure data
- Approved indicator definitions
- R4/R5/R6 source data where those releases are included
- Reporting authority and export policy

## Exit criteria

- Source and reported values reconcile
- Approver, submitter and return/revision roles are enforced
- Reporting remains organization-scoped
- Export fields and rows are approved and audited
