# Release R6 - Activities and camps

## Objective

Manage approved activities, camps and community-service participation.

## Scope

- Activity/camp lifecycle
- Organizing structures and responsible adults
- Registration, capacity and waiting lists
- Attendance and adult teams
- Participation authorizations
- Resources and reports

## Dependencies

- R1 structure/member model
- R2 mandate/scope model
- Safeguarding and guardian authorization policy
- Approved activity, camp and incident rules

## Exit criteria

- Responsible-role authority is enforced
- Minor and guardian authorization is checked where approved
- Unrelated minor and participant data is inaccessible
- Incident and health data remain disabled until separately approved
- Attendance and reports reconcile
